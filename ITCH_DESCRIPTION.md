## Body

**Skirmishers of SPLORR!!**

A hex-and-counter wargame for one player. You command a band of kobolds against a computer army on a 20 by 20 hex map of grass, forest, hills, roads, rivers and walls.

Plays in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone, with keyboard, mouse, gamepad or touch.

**How it plays**

- **Dice.** Attackers roll attack dice, defenders roll defence dice. White dice score 1 in 3, red 1 in 2, black 2 in 3. Every hit not blocked costs a body point, and wounds stay.
- **Ten kinds of kobold.** Daggers, slings, axes, swords, bows, crossbows that pierce armour, pikes that strike first when an enemy steps next to them, fast scouts, sappers that break walls into rubble, and the Wololo, who blesses friends and curses enemies.
- **Armour.** Leather, chain and plate add defence dice.
- **Ground matters.** Forest is slow and blocks shots. Roads are quick. High ground gives advantage in melee and longer shots. Shallow water is slow, deep water and walls cannot be crossed, and a unit behind a wall has cover.
- **Fog of war.** You see only what your kobolds see. Enemies hide in the woods until you are close, and the computer knows no more than its own units have seen.
- **Fortify.** A unit can dig in instead of moving: until your next turn, every failed defence die is rolled again.
- **A computer opponent** that holds out of reach, takes high ground and forest, and picks its targets.

**Scenarios**

Fifteen battles: four against four on open, wooded or hilly ground; the nine-a-side Kobold Warband; Shield Wall vs Skirmishers, Champion's Guard vs the Horde, Ambush in the Woods and Archers on the Heights; River Crossing and Walled Farmsteads; and two battles to watch the computer fight itself.

**Custom battle:** pick the forest, road, hills, water and walls, then give each side a ready-made army, a random one, or one you buy unit by unit within a points budget.

**Controls**

| | Keyboard | Gamepad | Mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Move the cursor | arrows or WASD | D-pad or stick | point | tap a hex |
| OK | Space or Enter | A | click | tap the same hex again |
| Cancel / back | Esc or Backspace | B | Menu button | Menu button |
| Menu | Tab or P | Start | Menu button | Menu button |
| Zoom | + / - / 0 | LB / RB | wheel | pinch |

Your next unit is selected for you. Yellow hexes show where it can move and a red ring marks an enemy it can attack. OK on the selected unit opens its menu (Fortify, Finish move, Chant). The turn ends by itself once none of your units can act. In the menu, "Let the computer finish" plays out the battle at fast speed. F finishes a move, C aims a Wololo's chant, M switches sound.
