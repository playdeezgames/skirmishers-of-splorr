## Body

**Skirmishers of SPLORR!!**

A hex-and-counter wargame for one player. You command a band of kobolds against a computer army on a 20 by 20 hex map of grass, forest and road.

**How it plays**

- Attackers roll attack dice, defenders roll defence dice, and every hit not blocked costs a body point. Kobolds have one.
- Kobolds stab with daggers. Kobold Slingers sling from 2 or 3 hexes away, and fall back on their daggers up close.
- Forest is slow going and blocks sling shots. Roads are quick.
- Each unit moves, then may attack. Or it can fortify and dig in: until your next turn, every failed defence die is rolled again.

**Controls**

Click a unit to select it, then click a yellow hex to move or a red-outlined enemy to attack. D fortifies, F finishes a unit's move, Space ends the turn, N starts a new game, and [ ] changes scenario.

**Scenarios**

Four against four on light woods or on maps that are 25, 50 or 75 percent forest, plus two battles to watch the computer fight itself, four against four and twenty against twenty.
